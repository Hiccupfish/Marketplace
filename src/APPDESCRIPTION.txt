Marketplace Business Description

Marketplace is a community-driven digital commerce platform that connects buyers, sellers, service providers, and delivery drivers in one trusted ecosystem. Designed with local communities in mind, the platform makes it easy to buy and sell products, hire skilled professionals, request services, and arrange secure deliveries—all within a single application.

Unlike traditional marketplaces, Marketplace introduces a competitive bidding model that allows users to negotiate the best value for every transaction. Buyers can receive multiple offers from sellers or service providers, while independent delivery drivers compete to transport purchased goods by submitting delivery bids based on price, vehicle type, estimated delivery time, and customer ratings. This creates a transparent environment where users choose the option that best meets their needs rather than accepting a fixed price.

Trust and safety are at the heart of the platform. Users progress through verification levels, build reputation through successful transactions, and earn community ratings that help others make informed decisions. Advanced moderation, identity verification, and a robust review system work together to reduce fraud and promote safe local commerce.

Marketplace supports four interconnected ecosystems:

Products Marketplace – Buy and sell new or second-hand products locally.
Services Marketplace – Hire trusted professionals such as mechanics, plumbers, painters, electricians, tutors, caterers, and other skilled artisans.
Delivery Marketplace – Independent drivers compete for delivery opportunities by submitting delivery offers, allowing buyers and sellers to choose the best combination of price, speed, vehicle, and reliability.
Requests Marketplace – Buyers post requests for products or services, allowing sellers and service providers to compete by submitting quotations or offers.

By combining verified identities, competitive bidding, community trust, and flexible logistics, Marketplace transforms local buying and selling into a safer, more transparent, and more efficient experience. Rather than being just another classifieds platform, it empowers communities by creating economic opportunities for entrepreneurs, service providers, and independent drivers while giving customers greater choice, better pricing, and increased confidence in every transaction.

Vision

To become Africa's most trusted community commerce platform, where every local entrepreneur, skilled professional, and independent driver has equal access to economic opportunities through secure, transparent, and technology-driven trade.

Mission

To empower local communities by connecting buyers, sellers, service providers, and delivery partners through a trusted digital marketplace that prioritises safety, affordability, reputation, and opportunity for all.
